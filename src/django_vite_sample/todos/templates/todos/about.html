About

These pages are rendered by Django templates. They manage the same todos as the Svelte app, but with plain HTML forms instead of the JSON API, so they work without JavaScript.

They have their own plum and rose palette, in todos/static/todos/site.css, so you can tell them apart from the Svelte pages.
